Seek listings read live on 2 Nov 2026; employer accreditation checked live on the INZ register the same day.

## Visa and pay

Software Engineer (ANZSCO 261313) is Green List Tier 1, Straight to Residence, in the 9 March 2026 Appendix 13. Pay condition: NZD 70.00 per hour (about NZD 145,600 a year), or NZD 87.50 per hour plus 10 years' experience on a contract for services. That page is marked archived, so confirm the current threshold. Most senior iOS roles pay NZD 130k to 160k, so a candidate may land below the Tier 1 line and rely on AEWV plus Skilled Migrant points instead.

## [B] Deep dives, top 3

### 1. Air New Zealand, Senior Mobile Engineer (iOS)

- Profile: national carrier, NZX-listed, large in-house digital team; the iOS app is a flagship consumer product.
- Requirements vs candidate: 5+ years iOS (match), deep Swift and SwiftUI (match), architecture and design patterns (VIPER is a talking point), CI/CD (match), AI tooling in workflow (state it), mentoring (highlight). AWS and backend-for-frontend are advantageous.
- Sponsorship and salary: not stated. Auckland market for this level is NZD 140k to 165k. Accredited, no visa statement; ask directly.
- Strategy: lead with regulated-fintech scale (uptime, security, release governance); frame eKYC identity flows as the analogue to passenger identity, check-in and payments; show CI/CD ownership with named tools.

### 2. Halter, Senior/Lead Mobile Engineer (iOS)

- Profile: Auckland agri-tech scale-up, venture-backed, hardware plus app. Mobile codebase spans React Native, Kotlin and Swift; offline-first is a stated plus.
- Requirements vs candidate: senior native iOS (match), cross-platform willingness (Flutter and Android transfer), performance and offline work, AI tooling (Claude, Cursor named), product sense, technical leadership.
- Sponsorship and salary: not stated. Scale-up band likely NZD 150k to 180k plus equity. Accredited, no visa statement.
- Strategy: emphasise multi-stack mobile; bring one concrete offline-first or unreliable-network story from fintech; show performance work with real measurements.

### 3. Absolute IT client, Senior iOS Engineer (iPad, Swift/Objective-C)

- Profile: unnamed "global technology organisation building scalable SaaS platforms", Auckland Central hybrid, recruiter-managed.
- Requirements vs candidate: proven senior iOS, Swift and Objective-C modernisation, iPad apps, API and cloud integration, AI dev tools, mentoring and code review. SwiftUI not mentioned.
- Sponsorship and salary: NZD 130k to 150k plus budgeted bonus. Ad text: the client "will sponsor ideal candidates who are already in New Zealand or plan to move to New Zealand at their own cost" and will "prioritise candidates already eligible to work in New Zealand".
- Strategy: state up front that relocation is self-funded and only AEWV is needed; highlight Objective-C to Swift and VIPER modernisation work; ask for the client name to verify accreditation.
