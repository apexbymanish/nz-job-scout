# NZ job scout: .NET, remittance and fintech roles, 13 November 2026

Seek listings read live on 13 Nov 2026 (about 470 ads from 20+ keyword searches; each ad's full text was scanned for .NET/C#, fintech/payments terms and visa wording). Employers were checked live on the INZ accredited employer register the same day. Xe's own careers portal was read directly.

**Caveats first**

- **Your CV has no commercial .NET work.** It is all iOS/Swift (plus Flutter). Every role below is a backend or full-stack .NET/C# job. Recruiters will screen on years of C#. Your best pitch is domain fit (remittance, KYC, payments), not .NET depth. Only apply claiming .NET skill you can show (a GitHub project, GME backend work you touched).
- **No live .NET or fintech ad said "AEWV sponsorship offered" outright.** Two ads came close: Propellerhead invites international applicants who relocate to Auckland, and an Absolute IT recruiter ad says the client will sponsor people moving at their own cost. Many ads said "no sponsorship" (Datacom's other ads, Theta, Randstad, Ocean IT, Enable NZ).
- "VERIFIED INZ ACCREDITED" means the employer can legally sponsor. It does not mean they have offered to.
- On 13 Nov the INZ site showed a banner saying visa processing was paused because of a global Microsoft outage. Check the status before you lodge anything.

## [B] Deep dives, top 3

### 1. Xe, Full Stack Software Engineer (Christchurch)

1. **Company:** Global FX and cross-border payments business (consumer and business money transfer in NZ, AU, UK, CA, US). Christchurch is a major engineering site.
2. **Requirements vs you:** 3-6 years of production web apps end to end; Vue/React frontend; C#/.NET backend preferred (Java, Kotlin, Go welcome); SQL; AWS (SQS, DynamoDB, S3, ECS, Lambda); daily agentic coding tools. Nice-to-have: payments/FX/regulated industry, CI/CD. **Strong match** on domain (KYC, payment initiation, FX, remittance compliance), CI/CD and Claude Code. **Gap:** web frontend, C# backend and AWS are not on your CV.
3. **Sponsorship and salary:** No visa or salary statement. Software Engineer 261313 is Green List Tier 1 with a pay condition of NZD 70/hour in the 9 Mar 2026 list; a mid-level full-stack role in Christchurch is unlikely to reach that, so expect AEWV first.
4. **Positioning:** (a) Lead with GME Remit: you have shipped onboarding, eKYC, FX rates and remittance flows in a licensed money-transfer app, which is exactly their domain list. (b) Show daily Claude Code use with your shared team commands; the ad names Claude Code. (c) Close the stack gap honestly: name any backend/API work you did at GME, and put a small C#/.NET + Vue or React project on GitHub before applying.

### 2. Propellerhead, Senior Software Engineer (Auckland)

1. **Company:** Auckland software consultancy building enterprise and government systems; recruits internationally and runs a relocation site (workingforpropellerhead.co.nz).
2. **Requirements vs you:** Senior engineer in the CTO's core team, guiding proofs of concept to production, mentoring, secure enterprise architecture "in the age of AI". Stack spans .NET, Java, TypeScript, React, Azure, AWS. Match: seniority, mentoring, AI tooling, release ownership. Gap: enterprise backend and cloud.
3. **Sponsorship and salary:** Accredited; ad explicitly welcomes international applicants relocating to Auckland. No salary stated. Ad was marked "Expiring": apply now.
4. **Positioning:** (a) Frame the CocoaPods-to-SPM/Tuist migration as leading a technical strategy change across a team. (b) Show you introduced AI-assisted development to the iOS team. (c) Ask whether they also hire senior mobile engineers; a consultancy may place you on a mobile engagement.

### 3. Absolute IT, Senior Software Engineer (Auckland, client unnamed)

1. **Company:** Global SaaS client investing in AI-driven products; multiple senior hires.
2. **Requirements vs you:** Strong full-stack plus practical AI development. C#, Java, Go, React, TypeScript, cloud. Match: AI tooling, seniority. Gap: full-stack web.
3. **Sponsorship and salary:** Up to NZD 150k plus bonus. Client will sponsor people who move at their own cost, which is your plan. Same recruiter firm as the Senior iOS ad in the 2 Nov report.
4. **Positioning:** (a) Mention you already applied for their Senior iOS role and are open to either. (b) Lead with AI-native work (Claude Code agent pack on GitHub). (c) Ask for the client name so you can check the register.
