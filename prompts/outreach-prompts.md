# Outreach prompts

Use these with the CV in `cv/` on hand. Replace the bracketed parts.

## Cover letter for a named role

```
Write a 250-word cover letter for the [ROLE TITLE] role at [COMPANY] ([job board + listing id]).
Candidate: Senior iOS engineer, 7 years in mobile, the last 4 leading the iOS app at GME Remit
(licensed cross-border remittance, South Korea). Needs AEWV sponsorship, relocating to [CITY] at own cost.
Stack: Swift, SwiftUI, VIPER, MVVM, Flutter/Dart, RESTful APIs, fastlane CI/CD, Tuist, SPM.
Domain: eKYC face verification, biometric login, payments, 25-language localisation, Korean financial compliance.
Structure: (1) one line on why this company's product; (2) three proof points mapping fintech reliability,
identity flows and release ownership to [COMPANY]'s domain; (3) one line on mentoring and AI-assisted tooling;
(4) a direct, polite line asking whether the role supports an Accredited Employer Work Visa.
Tone: concise, no buzzwords, no invented numbers.
```

## Email to a recruiter who already states sponsorship

```
Write a 150-word email to [RECRUITER NAME] at [AGENCY] for the [ROLE] role (ref [REF]).
Say: senior iOS engineer (Swift, Objective-C modernisation, VIPER, CI/CD), 7 years in mobile, 4 in regulated
fintech; based in Seoul; will relocate to [CITY] at own cost; needs AEWV sponsorship, which the ad says the
client offers; ask for the client's name to confirm accreditation on the INZ register; CV attached;
ask for a 15-minute call this week (KST, UTC+9, overlaps NZ mornings).
```

## CV tailoring for an accredited employer with no live role

```
Rewrite my CV summary and top 5 bullet points for a Senior Mobile Engineer role at [COMPANY], New Zealand.
Constraints: NZ CV style (2 pages, no photo, no date of birth, no nationality; one line on work rights:
"requires AEWV sponsorship, Green List Tier 1 occupation 261313").
Map my fintech experience to [COMPANY]'s domain: [one line on their product].
Quantify only what I can prove: commits, codebase size, languages supported, release cadence.
Lead with Swift/SwiftUI/VIPER, then Flutter/Dart, then APIs and CI/CD.
Include one bullet each on eKYC/compliance work and on fastlane release-pipeline ownership.
```
